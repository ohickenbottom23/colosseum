# Product background

## Who it's for

College students, 18 to 24, who live near their friends and are in a group chat all day. They already make casual calls about each other ("you're not making it to the gym four times this week") but nothing ever gets settled and nobody remembers who was actually right.

What they want out of it is status: being proven right in front of the people whose opinion matters to them. It's not about winning stuff.

## Positioning

"Everyone else built a scoreboard for other people's games. Colosseum builds one for your own."

What makes it different:
- Calls are about your friends' lives, not sports. The content comes from the group itself.
- Glory is a permanent record you can't buy or spend. It's closer to a chess rating than a wallet.
- Tokens are the risk dial and Glory is the trophy. Keeping them separate keeps the tension without it turning into gambling.

"No real money" and "bet on anything" are NOT selling points. Every competitor says both.

## Competitors

- bets (social betting app): closest to us. Calls about goals and dares between friends, no money. Small and unpolished.
- Poll: custom calls shared into iMessage group chats with points. Resolves by creator decision, an oracle, or a group vote. Worth copying how easy it is to share into a group chat.
- WagerLab: biggest free one. Sports first, virtual units, balances can be reset.
- youbetme: handshake bets, streaks, video "receipts" of the outcome
- Fliff / ReBet: social sportsbooks with two currencies. Fliff lets you buy coins. That's exactly the line we don't cross.
- The real competitor is the group chat itself. People already argue there for free.

## Known risks (keep these in mind when building)

- Gambling optics: never sell tokens, never use betting words in the UI. Pot splitting is the most casino-like thing we have, so describe it plainly.
- Resolution is the hardest part. People don't notice the conflict of interest until it hits them.
- Consent: calls about a person can feel targeted. The subject can always delete one about them.
- Cold start: the app is useless with one user. Inviting friends has to be easy.
- Retention: prediction apps spike and die. Still figuring out what brings people back every day.

## Monetization (later, not V1)

Never anything that touches tokens. Maybe cosmetics, private leagues, or a paid tier for big groups.
